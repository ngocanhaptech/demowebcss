Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered.

Quick answers for your first steps with Tripo 3D and AI-generated models.

I have no 3D modeling experience. Where should I start?

Start with the Image-to-3D workflow: choose an image with a clear subject and a relatively clean background, upload it to Tripo Studio, generate a model, review the result and then explore export options.

How can I improve the quality of a generated model?

Use a clear reference image with even lighting, provide multiple angles when appropriate, and review the mesh and textures after generation. Retopology and material refinement can help prepare assets for specific production workflows.

Which export formats can be used in other 3D applications?

Tripo tutorials discuss formats including USD, FBX, OBJ, STL, GLB and 3MF. The best option depends on the target application, such as a game engine, browser-based viewer, DCC tool or 3D printing workflow.

Can I use generated models in commercial projects?

Usage rights depend on the plan and applicable terms. Review Tripo's current licensing and pricing information before using generated assets commercially.
